# Moderation and correction policy

No candidate auto-publishes. A moderator must answer:

1. Is the subject explicitly named in the source?
2. Is the source credible and publicly accessible?
3. Is the entity clearly identifiable?
4. Is it an existing entity or alias?
5. Is legal/investigative status represented exactly?
6. Does wording distinguish allegation, charge, plea and conviction?
7. Are dates accurate?
8. Is there a direct source URL?
9. Is the reported conduct actually connected to illegal wildlife trade?
10. Would publication cause an avoidable false-positive or identity risk?

Prefer courts, prosecutors and enforcement agencies, then government releases, major media, reputable investigative NGOs, and other corroborated OSINT. Do not infer relationships. Preserve caveats and conflicting/later outcomes. Reject anonymous accusations, name-only ambiguity, inaccessible evidence, irrelevant offenses, and unnecessary sensitive information.

## Corrections and removals

Triage promptly. Freeze or remove a record where credible evidence shows mistaken identity or material inaccuracy. Verify against primary sources, record the reason in the pull request, correct aliases/status/events/sources without erasing relevant historical status, rerun validation/tests, and reply to the requester with the commit. A source disappearing alone does not prove inaccuracy, but replace dead links where an authoritative archive or current source exists. Escalate close legal/identity calls to the repository owner.
